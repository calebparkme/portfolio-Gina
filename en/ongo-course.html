NVC
Philosophy

Can we live peacefully
beyond the workshop?

In learning spaces, we experience hearts opening and connecting. But when we return to daily life — at home, at work, in the middle of conflict — that sense fades. Life outside the classroom is always more complex, more irregular, more human.

This course is designed to actually live NVC and mindfulness within that 'everyday messiness'. The Ongo Book 2.0 helps you embody habits of peace and compassion through sustained inspiration, step-by-step guidance, and community support.

This book invites you to prioritize the meditative values of compassion and clarity in ordinary daily life. It is full of practical advice.

— Sharon Salzberg, Lovingkindness author
Peace

Peace

The practice of maintaining inner stillness even in the chaos of daily life. Through stillness, speech, and action meditation, peace becomes a way of living.

Wisdom

Wisdom

You free yourself from habitual thought patterns and learn to listen to the signals of body and emotion. Awareness opens the door to wisdom.

Compassion

Compassion

Beginning with self-compassion, through empathic listening, honest speaking, making requests, and forgiveness — compassion transforms relationships.

How It Works

🧘
Solo Practice

Daily personal meditation, journaling, and everyday experiments. Life itself becomes the practice ground.

🤝
Buddy Practice

Empathy practice with a partner or one colleague. Deep listening and honest dialogue are shared together.

⭕
Group Circle

A circle gathering where a small group comes together to share, support each other's practice, and experience community.

Who It's For

Who Can Join

New to NVC

Those encountering NVC for the first time or wanting to learn systematically from the ground up. You will naturally absorb it through practice, not theory.

Already Learned NVC

Those who've received NVC training but feel it doesn't translate into daily life. This course bridges the gap between knowing and living.

Couples & Families

Couples or families seeking deeper connection and understanding in their relationship. Grow together through buddy practice.

Small Groups & Communities

Communities — churches, workplaces, schools — wanting to build a nonviolent culture as a small group. Full group circle materials included.

About the Instructor

김현희 (Gina Hyunhee Kim)

As a Certified Logotherapist and Mindful Dialogue Practitioner, Gina has been offering integrated counseling combining NVC and Viktor Frankl's Logotherapy since 2020. Alongside crisis intervention for high-risk individuals facing severe depression, divorce, and suicidal ideation, she facilitates NVC group workshops to help participants practice empathic listening and self-compassion in daily life.

She holds a B.A. in English Literature from Hankuk University of Foreign Studies and her Logotherapist certification from the Korea Logotherapy Association. Through diverse ministry — Syrian refugee work, short-term missions in Mexico, China, and Canadian Indigenous communities — she continues sharing the language of empathy and connection.
